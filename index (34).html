How the live demo works

Only verified residents can join. In the real app your house number is checked against the guardhouse resident list. Here, anyone who enters the same street code joins the same street. Street activity clears itself after about 12 hours.

  1. Join on your phone or laptop.
  2. Tap Invite neighbours and let friends scan the QR code.
  3. Send a Salam Pagi, ask for help, or hold SOS. Everyone sees it live.
  4. One person joins as Security desk to run the console.
